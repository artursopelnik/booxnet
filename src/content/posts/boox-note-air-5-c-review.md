Announced in October 2025, the **BOOX Note Air 5 C** is an iterative update – and that's fine, because the recipe was already close to ideal. What matters is where Onyx invested: software longevity, the pen, and accessories.

## First E-Ink Tablet on Android 15

The Note Air 5 C was the first e-paper tablet to ship with Android 15, and Onyx promises **three years of software support** – a first for the category and a big deal for anyone burned by abandoned e-readers.

Despite hardware similar to its predecessor (2.4 GHz octa-core, 6 GB RAM, 64 GB storage), the Air 5 C feels noticeably smoother than the Note Air 4 C did at launch. Android 15 optimizations do real work here.

## Display

The 10.3-inch Kaleido 3 panel renders text at 300 PPI in black and white and color at 150 PPI with 4,096 colors. As with every Kaleido screen, the color filter darkens the background slightly in ambient light – the front light compensates well.

## The New Pen 3

The bundled **Pen 3** has a felt-like tip that adds just the right friction, and hides a clever party trick: the cap unscrews to reveal storage for three replacement nibs. Small thing, huge quality-of-life win.

## Keyboard Cover via Pogo Pins

A new pogo-pin connector snaps the optional Magnetic Keyboard Cover on instantly – no Bluetooth pairing, no charging. It turns the Air 5 C into a distraction-free writing machine.

## Verdict

At **$529 with the pen included**, the Note Air 5 C is the most complete color E-Ink notebook on the market. Owners of the Air 4 C can skip it; everyone else gets the safest recommendation in the category.

**Rating: 9/10**
