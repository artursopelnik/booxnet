Two of the most popular E-Ink notebooks go head to head: the **BOOX Note Air3 C** with its color display and full Android, against the **Supernote A5 X2** with its focused writing experience.

## Display

The Note Air3 C uses a 10.3-inch Kaleido 3 color display (300 PPI B&W / 150 PPI color). The Supernote A5 X2 stays monochrome but delivers outstanding contrast and a very paper-like surface.

If you need color for PDFs, annotations, or comics, BOOX wins. If you exclusively write and read text, Supernote's panel is arguably the nicer monochrome screen.

## Writing Feel

Supernote's self-recovering film layer combined with its ceramic-tipped pen is legendary – it is the closest thing to pen on paper in the industry. The Note Air3 C is good, but the glass surface feels slightly slicker.

## Software Philosophy

This is where the two devices diverge completely:

- **BOOX:** Full Android with Play Store. Install anything – Kindle, OneNote, Obsidian. Endless flexibility, more distraction.
- **Supernote:** A closed, focused system. Excellent note organization, linked notes, and calendar – but no third-party apps.

## Battery and Build

Both easily last one to two weeks of note-taking. The Note Air3 C's aluminum body is thinner; the Supernote feels sturdier in hand.

## Verdict

Choose the **Note Air3 C** if you want one device for everything: color reading, apps, notes. Choose the **Supernote A5 X2** if writing is your priority and distraction-free focus matters more than flexibility.
