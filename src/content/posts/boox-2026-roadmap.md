2026 is shaping up to be a year of iteration rather than reinvention for Onyx. Based on official teasers and consistent reporting, here is what the BOOX lineup should look like this year.

## Palma 3: Back to Black and White?

The rumor mill points to a **Palma 3** that returns to a monochrome E Ink display – a deliberate contrast to the color-equipped Palma 2 Pro. Pricing is expected to stay **below $300**, repositioning the standard Palma as the affordable pocket reader while the Pro carries the premium features.

## Poke 7 Launches May 21

Onyx has confirmed the **Poke 7** e-reader with a traditional black-and-white e-paper display. It launched in China on **May 21, 2026**, with an international release expected to follow.

## Go, Tab and Note Series Refreshes

Expect the familiar cadence:

- **Go series:** modest internal upgrades following the Go 10.3 Gen II and Go Tablet 10.3 / 10.3 Lumi releases
- **Tab X C:** an updated large-format color flagship
- **Note Air 6 / Note Air 6C:** the next iteration of the 10.3-inch notebook line

## Android 16 Becomes Standard

After the Note Air 5 C pioneered Android 15 on e-paper, **Android 16 is expected to become standard** on most new 2026 devices – continuing Onyx's newfound commitment to current software and multi-year update promises.

## Our Take

Iteration is not a bad thing. The BOOX lineup's weak spot was never hardware ambition – it was software support. Three-year update promises and current Android versions address exactly the right problem.
