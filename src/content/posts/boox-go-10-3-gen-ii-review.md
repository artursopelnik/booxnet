The **BOOX Go 10.3 Gen II** is Onyx's answer to everyone who liked the idea of a reMarkable or Kindle Scribe but didn't want to live inside a walled garden. The second generation keeps the thin-and-light formula and fixes the first generation's biggest weakness: outdated software.

## What's New in Gen II

- **Android 15** instead of Android 12, with a commitment to three years of firmware updates
- New **InkSense Plus stylus** with 4,096 pressure levels and 25 ms latency
- A **Lumi variant** that adds an adjustable dual-tone front light in a 4.8 mm body
- Same 10.3-inch, 300 PPI E Ink Carta 1200 monochrome display

## Display and Reading

The Carta 1200 panel delivers crisp, genuinely paper-like text. There is no color layer here – and that is a feature, not a bug: without the Kaleido color filter, the background is noticeably brighter than on color BOOX tablets.

The base model has no front light at all, which purists love for its clean look. Most people should spend the extra $30 on the Lumi.

## Writing

The InkSense Plus stylus is a real upgrade. 25 ms latency puts it in the same league as the reMarkable Paper Pro, and the slightly textured surface gives strokes a controlled, pencil-like resistance.

## Performance

The Snapdragon 750G (2.07 GHz) with 4 GB RAM won't win benchmarks, but for notes, reading apps, and split-screen PDF work it is consistently smooth. Android 15 brings noticeably better app compatibility than the Gen 1's Android 12.

## Price and Verdict

At **$419.99** (standard) and **$449.99** (Lumi), the Go 10.3 Gen II undercuts most premium competitors while offering the open Android ecosystem none of them have.

**Rating: 8.5/10** – the best open-platform E-Ink notebook in its price class.
