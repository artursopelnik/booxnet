The original Palma became a cult hit as the "phone that can't distract you." The **BOOX Palma 2 Pro** takes that formula and adds three things nobody expected in a pocket e-reader: a color screen, stylus support, and a SIM slot with 5G data.

## The Color Screen

The 6.13-inch **Kaleido 3** panel renders black-and-white content at 824 × 1648 (300 PPI) and color at 150 PPI with 4,096 colors. The screen sits flush under glass and reads beautifully in direct sunlight.

The trade-offs are the usual Kaleido ones: the color filter makes plain text look slightly grainier than on the monochrome Palma 2, and the base background is a touch darker. Comics, book covers, and web content gain a newspaper-like color tone that monochrome fans will still envy.

## 5G and the Hybrid SIM Slot

This is the headline feature. A hybrid SIM tray means the Palma 2 Pro works as a standalone connected device – maps, messaging, streaming audiobooks – without tethering to a phone. Alternatively, use the slot for expandable storage.

## Stylus Support

Handwriting on a 6-inch screen sounds odd, but for quick margin notes and to-do lists it genuinely works. The stylus is sold separately, which stings at this price.

## Verdict

The Palma 2 Pro turns the pocket e-reader into a smarter daily carry without losing its minimalist charm. It is however $120 more than the standard Palma 2, and the stylus and flip case cost extra. Buy it if color and cellular matter to you; stick with the regular Palma 2 if you just want distraction-free reading.

**Rating: 8/10**
